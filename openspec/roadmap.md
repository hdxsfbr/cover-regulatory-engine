This roadmap supersedes the previous incremental sequence as of 2026-10-22.
The complete contract is [docs/prd-delivery-plan.md](../docs/prd-delivery-plan.md),
traced to all four pages of the original PRD, including all six bonus features
requested for this full-feature delivery.

## Current state

The initial assessment change is archived. `live-parcel-assessment` and
`applicability-evidence` have completed component tasks and await synchronization/
archive. They are working foundations, not evidence of full product completion.
General automatic map interpretation, substantive ADU/guest-house support,
source-driven general rule synthesis and the bonus workflows remain incomplete.

## Scope and timebox

All LA residential searches use the general evidence path. Numerical regulatory
coverage remains an explicit source-reviewed subset, as the PRD permits. No
per-address seed file may substitute for general extraction. An always-unsupported
project type does not satisfy positive support for the three named types.

The original week remains the POC target. The delivery plan records an aggressive
five-day sequence and a more credible 10–15 focused-day forecast with early
feasibility gates. A missed timebox is reported, never silently waived or hidden
by cutting required features. Actual production deployment and independent
professional legal validation remain separately identified activities; neither
is a reason to defer the full POC feature set indefinitely.

## Release rule

Every PRD matrix row needs linked acceptance evidence and every release gate must
be checked. Passing unit tests and completed OpenSpec tasks are insufficient on
their own. The prior roadmap is retained in
`changes/prd-complete-delivery/prior-roadmap.md` for traceability.
